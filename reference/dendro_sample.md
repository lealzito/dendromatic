# Synthetic example dendrometer dataset

Two fully synthetic hourly stem-dendrometer series spanning two growing
seasons (April 2021 to November 2022, UTC), for examples and vignettes.
`SYN_Fs001` is a clean series (seasonal growth, diel shrink-recover
cycle, winter shrinkage). `SYN_Pa002` additionally carries a +35
micrometre sensor-reinstallation step on 2022-06-15 and a four-day data
gap in August 2021 – the two artefacts
[`dm_clean_series()`](https://lealzito.github.io/dendromatic/reference/dm_clean_series.md)
exists to fix.

## Usage

``` r
dendro_sample
```

## Format

A data.frame with 29,232 rows and 3 columns:

- series:

  character, series identifier (`SITE_SpAb###` style).

- ts:

  POSIXct timestamp, UTC, hourly.

- value:

  numeric, stem radius reading in micrometres.

## Source

Synthetic; generated by `data-raw/dendro_sample.R` (seed 421).
