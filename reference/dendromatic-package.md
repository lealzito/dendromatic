# dendromatic: Processing, Cleaning and Normalization of Dendrometer Time Series

Tools for point-dendrometer data pipelines: ingest raw logger tables of
any shape into the 'treenetproc' L0 format, process to L1/L2, correct
sensor relocations and jumps with growth-aware level shifting, erase
dormancy periods and align growing seasons, merge consecutive series
from the same tree, extract predawn and midday tree water deficit
extrema, and match leaf water potential campaigns to stem features. The
computational core behind the 'DendroMatic' Shiny applications, usable
standalone in scripted workflows.
